Next ownership change (proposal, not implemented)

PreparedPart owns immutable resources; legacy PartFrame owns cached material resolution; RigidBatch has stable CacheIndex but frame-local PacketIndex; voxel Parts own Mesh until replacement/removal. WorldPacket has no stable recipe handle. NativeWorldBlob serializes the full resource schema every frame despite those lifetimes. Map these owners to validated native recipe handles and send compact dynamic spans/counts/current view metadata, independent of candidate order and physical batch cardinality. Multiple physical draws may share an immutable recipe; rigid/generic pipelines need distinct recipes.

Use generation-bearing bounded handles and per-dependent-resource invalidation, instead of invalidating all recipes when any resource epoch changes. Per-slot material epochs still protect completion/reset/growth. Publish/retire transactionally, reject stale handles, clear identities on context/world reset, preserve current highlight/exclusion draw indices and retain a full validated legacy fallback. Do not reintroduce whole ordered-vector matching, per-frame object hashes or rejected byte-fill workers. Actual-path evidence must establish gains before retention. This native subset alone does not close the total-frame budget or visual parity gaps.
