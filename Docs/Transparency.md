# Phase #4: transparency, decals, flares and GPU particles

The native Vulkan 1.4 backend executes the installed SE 1.210.014 glass, decal, billboard, OIT and GPU-particle shaders. Published environment probes and later billboard composition are connected. Controlled GPU/game evidence establishes specific contracts; broad visual parity remains separate acceptance work. See `LightingParity.md` for current probe, material and lighting evidence and its limits.

## Shader and frame contracts

`scripts/generate-transparency-variants.py` selects authenticated catalog variants. Five glass and five dedicated hologram streams cover rigid, 60-matrix skinning, generic/static, packed cube and deformed cube geometry. Holo retains the stock texture amplification and original-map binding; it never substitutes the glass pixel shader. Pass 5 writes weighted OIT; pass 6 writes depth/normals for glass decals. Seven glass and seven dedicated Holo pairs join the eight existing world pairs in the offline supplement: **44 supplemental stages**, authenticated by binary/metadata/definition hashes. Startup does not launch a shader compiler.

Thirty-two billboard pairs combine lit, soft, alpha cutout, single channel and OIT/additive settings. BC4 red-only textures select stock `.xxxx` sampling; ordinary BC6H textures do not. Parent transforms, copied shared/persistent packets, color/intensity, per-billboard cutout/reflectivity/soft distance, UVs and standard/additive-bottom/additive-top ordering are preserved. LDR renders after tone mapping and before FXAA; PostPP renders after upscaling. Both retain submission order. The producer supports 32 custom projection slots and per-bucket render settings; those features still require broader matched-scene acceptance. The installed stock renderer does not enable its dormant REFLECTIVE billboard macro.

Opaque decals modify the source three-attachment GBuffer before lighting. Five stock material roles cover color, normal, combined color/normal, extensions and transparent color. Copied topology, parent IDs, material dictionaries, updates, fade/removal, render distance, quality-scaled queue limits, bone/cube attachments and parent removal belong to the managed scene. The native engine batches at most 512 decals per material; explicit packet/material/residency limits fail with actionable diagnostics.

Authored mesh `DECAL_CUTOUT` parts use the stock alpha clip and `+0.0001` clip-Z offset with read-only depth. OLD rigid, skinned, generic, cube and deformed-cube streams have dedicated pipelines 22–31, including metalness-colorable variants; GPU-owned OLD families and managed/retained fallback select the same technique. GPU cutout batches follow all solid batches, including across geometry pages. Cutouts remain excluded from stock depth and forward passes. `Tests/StaticDecalGpuTests` reproduces surface detail lost by the ordinary OLD shader, checks alpha holes and read-only depth at several distances, and compares resident rigid/generic/cube/deformed-cube pixels and shadow coverage against stock-stream controls in both frame slots. The reported room screenshot still needs a live comparison after deploying and restarting.

Glass decals preserve the stock source-pipeline split. OLD glass enters the two distance groups only when its owner has registered decals and its actor distance squared is below 10000. The groups retain one depth copy, disable the fragment shader and leave copied normals unchanged. The installed OLD skip/count selection bound is preserved. NEW glass has two independent opaque-depth copies and filtered depth/normal layers. Its decals use the current layer as both sampled depth and the read-only depth attachment; OLD decals retain the opaque attachment. Glass and particles keep opaque depth read-only. Frame order is GBuffer/decal lighting, additive-bottom, standard billboards/particles, OLD glass and its decals, NEW glass and its decals, OIT resolve, additive-top, tone mapping, LDR, FXAA/postprocessing, upscale, PostPP and UI. Native counters distinguish submitted glass, layers, decals and billboards. Complex overlapping glass still needs matched live comparison; the focused source-selection controls alone do not establish that acceptance.

## Particle ownership and scheduling

The stock CPU effect manager retains definitions, curves, emitter allocation, gravity, effect state and removal callbacks. Installed IL fingerprints, runtime signatures and competing Harmony owners are checked before activation. All 56 native entry points are checked before API/device use. Three narrowly scoped hooks replace the D3D atlas update and particle-light update/destruction. Fingerprints encode floating-point operands as exact IEEE bits so Legacy and Interim agree. Particle lights register under API-neutral `MyLightComponent`, as the stock actor lookup requires.

Ten authenticated GPU stages implement dead-list initialization/reset, emission and skip correction, alternating alive lists, indirect simulation arguments, simulation and indirect vertex/pixel rendering. A stock emitter is copied as 464 bytes; no native pointer survives the queue call. The renderer supports 1024 emitter slots and 409600 particles. A stable texture-array index mapping lasts through the scene and clears on unload. Stock texture data is normalized into a Vulkan array through the existing upload/retirement mechanism.

Frame-slot buffers and barriers order compute, indirect arguments, particle rendering and readback. Counter status reads only already-completed slots and never waits for GPU work. Paused simulation supplies zero frame delta. Expired particles recycle into the dead list; scene reset clears lifetime state. The stock allocator's dead-list sentinel and rounded dispatch accesses require explicitly enabled robust buffer access. Weighted OIT requires independent attachment blending. The required feature mask is **0x3C3F**, with ten used bits; optional host copying/local reads remain optional.

## Flares

Copied light glare descriptions retain direction/distance fades, intensity/occlusion curves, ghost placement and glare types. A native compute query counts all covered pixel centers of the stock camera-facing query quad against opaque depth with strict reverse-depth comparison. The scene supplies its projected half extents and the stock radial-distance normalization area, including QueryShift. Samples outside the viewport never pass. Results retain completed-slot ownership; the scene submits additive-top flare billboards after the first completed query. Deleted owners release result state.

The legacy pixel-radius query entry remains available and converts into the same exact-coverage path. Completed results have up to two-frame latency. Managed query state now uses stock minimum/random retry intervals, the half-minimum first-visible retry, interpolation and immediate fully blocked results. Unique request identities reject stale completed-slot data; deleted lights release pending state. Rotated flares use the stock world-up quad helper. Additive-top billboards ignore opaque depth and soft-depth fade, separately from bottom/standard pipelines in both HDR formats. Broad lens-flare and overlapping transparency comparisons remain open.

## Validation

`Tests/TransparencyGpuTests` checks actual stock glass/OIT pixels, order independence, depth occlusion, instancing and resize; all 32 billboard pairs and BC4 channel semantics; decal material roles/fades/glass layers; completed flare visibility; particle emission, indirect pixels, expiry, slot reuse and reset. `Tests/ParticleBridgeTests` checks installed/mismatched hook IL, source glare curves, exact-type particle light registration/destruction and copied decal ownership. `Tests/RuntimeShaderTests` validates all 1401 executable modules, checking vertex binding relocation changes only descriptor-binding words and preserves all other instructions/interfaces/layouts.

Run the private game gate with `Tests/CreativeFixtureHarness/Run-Isolated.ps1 -Transparency -ObserveD3D11 -DurationSeconds 420`. It adds three window blocks, opaque/transparent decals, `Damage_Electrical_Damaged_Antenna`, `WM_RainHeavy` and `WM_SnowSlow` to the Creative fixture, captures held cameras, stops effects, removes decals and unloads. Add `-RenderDocDirectory` for native world/LCD/transparency/particle captures. Run the same seeded fixture with `-Backend Dx11` for comparison. `scripts/verify-transparency-fixture.py` validates markers, delivered captures, counters, errors, cleanup and the observer's limited scope. Captured weather is sparse; counter/command evidence does not establish precipitation pixel parity.

See `Validation.md` for concrete runs. Windows net48/net10 builds, Pulsar source compilation and Linux cross-builds are separate from live Interim/Linux game acceptance. Glass, billboard, particle and transparent-decal paths bind published environment probes, with ambient fallback until available; glass/particles also bind the installed BRDF LUT. Hardware probe filtering, particle lighting/collisions, distant/complex glass ordering, weather placement/quality, content mods and broad LDR/PostPP/stereo comparisons remain visual-acceptance limits.

NEW visible glass submission preserves the registered material's `TriangleFaceCulling` policy in both ordinary and native mixed-model paths. OLD visible glass selects the default clockwise back-cull rasterizer regardless of the material flag. Earlier always-two-sided claims were incorrect: `MyTransparentModelPass.Begin` selects null, which the context forwards to D3D11's default rasterizer. Both OLD `BeginDepthOnly` and NEW `TransparentForDecals` also choose default back-culling. The focused opposite-winding controls distinguish these policies. Live overlapping-glass appearance remains under investigation; see `LightingParity.md` for current evidence.

`DrawTransparentModels` gates OLD glass; `DrawTransparentModelsInstanced` independently gates NEW glass, including the native mixed-model consumer and its managed fallback. Turning both off retains opaque parts. The actual scene/GPU regression toggles and restores these settings, compares source packets and visible pixels, and checks that ownership survives the handoff.

The 2026-10-18 coverage audit reran `BillboardPacketTests`: all 39 checks pass, including transient/persistent triangle ownership and PostPP submission order. `HudBillboardGpuTests` passes on RTX 5070 Ti: PostPP planes at Z -0.06/-0.05/-0.049999 produce 242/338/338 changed pixels, the production scene/material/texture path produces 338, and enabled validation reports zero errors. NGX modes were not requested in this rerun. These controls do not establish full LDR/custom-projection or scene parity. Logs: `TestResults/lighting-{billboard-packet,hud}-coverage.log`.
